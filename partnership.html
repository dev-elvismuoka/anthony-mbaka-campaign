People support people they understand.

The Steward of Muthwani

A Leader Shaped By Purpose. Driven By Impact.

Anthony Davids Mbaka is a systems strategist, entrepreneur, development advocate and community leader committed to building sustainable solutions for Muthwani Ward. He holds a Bachelor of Arts in International Relations and Diplomacy from United States International University-Africa and a professional certification in Flour Milling Operations from the African Milling School.

His professional journey includes experience in manufacturing, logistics, transportation, operations management and entrepreneurship through organizations such as Chandaria Industries, Bingwa Flour Mills, Roy Hauliers and Davids Travel House. These experiences shaped his belief that sustainable development is achieved through systems, accountability and measurable outcomes.

The Decision Set Before Me & The Closed Doors Story

Anthony’s journey is one of resilience. Throughout his career, he faced his share of "closed doors." But he realized that every closed door was not a rejection—it was preparation for a much larger responsibility. He chose public service because he looked at Muthwani Ward and saw incredible, untapped potential being suffocated by poor leadership.
