The Steward of Muthwani

A Leader Shaped by Purpose. Driven by Impact.

Anthony Davids Mbaka is a visionary leader, entrepreneur, and systems strategist committed to transformative development and sustainable growth. A BA Graduate in International Relations & Diplomacy from USIU-A and certified in Flour Milling Operations from The Africa Milling School, Anthony brings a rare combination of academic rigor, technical expertise, and operational excellence.

Over the years, Anthony has held key roles in Chandaria Industries, Bingwa (Grainden) Flour Mills, and Roy Hauliers, where he honed skills in resource management, operational efficiency, and strategic execution — experiences that have shaped his ability to build accountable, scalable systems that deliver tangible results.

As the founder of Davids Travel House, he has demonstrated entrepreneurial leadership and innovative problem-solving, proving that disciplined planning and execution can drive impact at scale.

Guided by the philosophy “Kazi Si Maneno | Leadership That Delivers,” Anthony is committed to creating responsive, transparent, and results-driven systems in Muthwani Ward, ensuring that every resident benefits from smart infrastructure, economic opportunity, and accountable leadership.

With deep empathy for the everyday realities of residents and a clear vision for unlocking Muthwani’s potential, Anthony’s approach resonates with corporate leaders, NGO executives, faith-based organizations, and community stakeholders seeking leaders who can translate vision into action.

SMART INFRASTRUCTURE REVOLUTION (SIR)

The Foundation of a Modern Muthwani.

The Smart Infrastructure Revolution (SIR) is the foundation of our development plan, the core system that unlocks mobility, growth, safety, and opportunity for every resident.

  • 🚆 Muthwani Metro Railway Transit Service: Our flagship project connecting Muthwani to Nairobi, via Embakasi terminus, and Syokimau terminus. Faster movement. Bigger opportunities.
  • 🛣️ Smart Roads & Mobility Network: Upgraded main and feeder roads across all estates and villages. Safe, efficient, reliable transport for work and business.
  • 💧 Water Security for Every Home: Expanded pipelines, borehole integration, and smart distribution through proper coordination between the MCA office, Mavwasco, Machakos County Government and Athi Water Works. Clean, dependable water as a basic right.
  • 💡 Smart Lighting & Public Safety: “Mulika Mwizi” LED solar lighting and modern safety tech. Secure neighbourhoods, vibrant evenings.
  • 🏫 Schools, Health & Public Facilities: Modern schools, a community hospital, dispensaries, and a digital library, communal public playgrounds and public spaces within our neighbourhoods. Strong institutions for a stronger community.

MUTHWANI FINANCIAL EMPOWERMENT PROGRAMME (MFEP)

Building Prosperity for Every Household

The Muthwani Financial Empowerment Programme (MFEP) strengthens our local economy by putting skills, income, and opportunity directly into the hands of our residents.

  • 💼 Enterprise & Small Business Support: Training, business clinics, and easier permits. Stronger businesses. More income.
  • 🧑‍🎓 Youth Skills & Employment: Modern skills training and job-linkage programmes. Empowered youth. Better jobs.
  • 👩‍👩‍👧 Community's Economic Growth: Stronger table-banking groups, cooperative support, and improved market centres. Community rising = families rising.
  • 🏢 Business & Opportunity Desk: A dedicated desk at the MCA office for investors and entrepreneurs. Faster approvals. More opportunities.
  • 📊 Transparent Budgeting: Open community budgeting and accountable fund usage. Every shilling works for the people.
  • 🌾 Food Security & Local Production: Support for farmers and modern techniques. Stable food. Stable incomes.

RESPONSIVE, ACCOUNTABLE, INNOVATIVE LEADERSHIP (RAIL)

Leadership That Listens, Acts, and Delivers

The RAIL Agenda is our governance commitment — ensuring leadership in Muthwani is responsive to people’s needs, accountable in service delivery, and innovative in solving everyday challenges.

  • 📢 Responsive Leadership: We will strengthen direct engagement with residents through open forums, feedback channels, and rapid response systems. Leadership that listens and acts.
  • 📊 Accountable Governance: Clear reporting, transparent use of public resources, and regular community updates on all development projects. Every action must be justified. Every shilling must be accounted for.
  • 💡 Innovative Solutions: Use of smart systems, data-driven planning, and modern tools to improve service delivery across the ward. Smarter systems. Better results.
  • 🤝 Citizen-Centered Service Delivery: Prioritizing residents’ needs in all decisions — from infrastructure to social services. People first, always.
  • ⚖️ Ethical & Results-Driven Leadership: Commitment to integrity, discipline, and measurable outcomes in all public work. No excuses. Only delivery.

STRATEGY
JAN 23, 2026

Building Systems That Deliver

My Commitment to Transforming Muthwani Ward and Strengthening Machakos County Governance. As Kenya heads toward the 2027 elections...

As Kenya heads toward the 2027 elections, our national and county conversations are shifting. Voters are demanding infrastructure that works, economic empowerment that creates opportunities, and leadership that delivers measurable results.

I am grounded in integrity, clarity, and a systems-driven approach to development.

As MCA, I will prioritize:

  • 》Upgrading and maintaining key road networks across all our neighbourhoods ensuring seamless connectivity from one neighbourhood to the next to create a web of opportunities.
  • 》Improving water supply systems for reliability and household affordability.
  • 》Enhancing drainage and walkways to schools for accessibility during rainy days.
  • 》Expanding street & market centres lighting and digital connectivity to support safety, trade, and education.

Economic Empowerment That Lifts Households

My economic empowerment agenda focuses on practical, sustainable impact:

  • ▪︎Strengthening SMEs and local enterprises.
  • ▪︎Supporting youth, women, PLWD, and artisans with access to skills and markets.
  • ▪︎Building partnerships that open pathways to digital work, agribusiness, and local manufacturing.
  • ▪︎Mobilizing community savings groups and micro-enterprise programs.

Our people want work and systems that support progress — not political rhetoric.

Education is the Bridge to Transformation

Education is the bridge to transformation, yet too many young people in Machakos are still left behind. Under my leadership:

  • ☆Every bursary shilling will be transparent and accounted for appropriately.
  • ☆We will look for internships, industry linkages, and career placements for all unemployed by developing a workforce employment online system for the residents.
  • ☆Community-based scholarships will support needy students.
  • ☆Digital learning and mentorship programs will be actively integrated across the Ward.

And most importantly: I will rally fellow Members of the Machakos County Assembly to legislate policies that anchor our education priorities into the County Integrated Development Plan (CIDP). This ensures long-term continuity, funding, and measurable indicators.

The Next Assembly Must:

  • ¤Strengthen oversight.
  • ¤Improve development planning.
  • ¤Enhance citizen participation.
  • ¤Demand accountability from the County Executive.
  • ¤Align legislation directly with real community needs.

I envision — and will help build — a vibrant Assembly where debates are intelligent, informed, and impact-oriented. An Assembly where motions, bills, and committee work genuinely to uplift Machakos residents.

-I come into this race not to add noise, but to provide solutions.

-Not to follow political trends, but to set new standards.

-Not to speak promises, but to build systems that deliver.

Muthwani deserves visible, consistent, accountable leadership — every day, not every five years. And I am ready to bring that leadership. Kazi Si Maneno | Leadership That Delivers.

COMMUNITY
JAN 23, 2026

Muthwani Ward in 2026: We Deserve Kazi Si Maneno

A message to the people of Joska Market, Utawala estates, Ng’alalya, Ivovoani, Katani, Seme, Manyatta, Mayland and Muthwani...

A message to the people of Joska Market, Utawala estates, Ng’alalya, Ivovoani, Katani, Seme, Manyatta, Mayland and Muthwani — your struggle is real, your voice matters, and 2027 can be our turning point.

For too long, we have woken up to the same hardships. Our children walk on dusty roads in the dry season and sink into mud in the rains. Market merchants in Joska Market, Utawala estates, Ng’alalya, Ivovoani, and Muthwani know the pain of moving goods because most roads are impassable. Public transport ends at Airways, Githunguri, Katani Quarry Road, and Kangundo Road, leaving residents to struggle on Boda bodas just to get home.

Water access from Mavwasco is stalled, leaving homes thirsty for reliable supply. Katani Level 2 Dispensary — meant to serve our community — stands in disarray, forcing mothers, children, and elders to travel far for even the simplest care. These aren’t distant stories — these are our daily realities.

But these daily struggles were not inevitable. They are the result of leadership that promised much but delivered little.

The Cost of Absentee Leadership

Our leaders in the Machakos County Assembly — including past MCAs like George Kingori (2017–2022) and earlier officeholders — sat in powerful committees meant to serve the people yet failed to leverage them for real development. These committees include:

🔹 House Business Committee: This is where important issues are scheduled for debate and action in the Assembly — issues like road repairs, transport access, school expansion, and water supply. Yet our matters sat too long on paper instead of being acted upon.

🔹 Sectoral Committees (Education, Roads, Health, Lands): These are the committees meant to oversee budgets, fast‑track projects, and enforce accountability. They should have ensured that your money translated into passable roads, functional schools, and reliable health centers.

But the results are in front of us — inadequate roads, broken health services, water shortages, and stalled school improvements. The tools were there. The power was there. But the commitment was not.

Committee Power Is Meant to Deliver Results

Today, Hon. Dominic Maitha serves in some of the most strategic committees — and this matters for you:

  • 🔹 Chairperson — Committee on Implementation: This committee’s job is to ensure that the County Assembly’s resolutions and Executive decisions are not just passed, but executed. It is where plans become action on the ground.
  • 🔹 Labour, Public Service, and ICT Committee: Oversees workers and service delivery systems — a backbone for efficient county services.
  • 🔹 Education Committee: Can help ensure that schools are expanded, adequately resourced, and accessible for our children.
  • 🔹 Lands and Energy Committee: Oversees electrification projects, land allocation, and infrastructure — key to expanding markets and homes.

Imagine if past leaders had used these same committees with seriousness and urgency. Roads would be smooth, buses would reach further into our neighbourhoods, clinics like Katani Dispensary would flourish, and water would reach every household.

Instead, we stood by as words filled the air but action failed to follow.

Why 2027 Must Be Our Turning Point

2027 is not just another election — it’s our moment of decisive choice. We need a leader who:

  • ✔ Understands House Business and Assembly procedures. Someone who doesn’t wait to be told what to do but pushes our urgent needs into the decision agenda.
  • ✔ Has a concrete plan READY from day one. No waiting, no delays — roads, schools, health facilities, water systems must be on the implementation schedule from the start.
  • ✔ Delivers on promises with urgency and accountability. It’s not enough to talk — we must see visible change in our daily lives.

This is the true meaning of Kazi Si Maneno | Leadership That Delivers.

A Call to the People of Muthwani Ward

2027 is the year we say:

  • 🔹 No more roads that trap our children in dust and mud.
  • 🔹 No more water shortages that dictate our day.
  • 🔹 No more health facilities that leave us unattended.
  • 🔹 No more residents walking long distances from Airways, Githunguri, Katani Quarry, or Kangundo Road.
  • 🔹 No more merchants in Joska Market and estates struggling to move goods to markets and customers.

We deserve leadership that doesn’t wait for development to filter down — leadership that forces it up and into our communities.

Together, Let’s Bring Real Change

Every election is a chance to choose what kind of future we want. In 2027, let the people of Joska Market, Utawala estates, Ng’alalya, Ivovoani, and Muthwani rise together and elect leadership with a tested plan and the will to implement it — a leader who understands House Business, committees, action, and accountability.

No more empty promises. No more excuses. Kazi Si Maneno | Leadership That Delivers.
