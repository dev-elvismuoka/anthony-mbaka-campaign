The Steward of Muthwani

A Leader Shaped by Purpose. Driven by Service.

Anthony Davids Mbaka is a visionary community leader and entrepreneur who seeks to serve as the next Steward of Muthwani Ward — a role he approaches with humility, clarity, and deep commitment. He is anchored in results.

Guided by the philosophy “Kazi Si Maneno | Leadership That Delivers,” Anthony is committed to building a Muthwani where opportunity, dignity, and development reach every home.

Having lived the everyday realities that face families across the ward, he understands the challenges and sees the untapped potential within our people. His life and work are built on discipline, excellence, systems design, resource management, and community-focused service.

"Anthony presents himself to you as a leader who listens, works, and delivers — a servant of the people and a champion of progress for every household in Muthwani Ward."

Anthony Davids Mbaka

Our Promise

A Future That Works

"Muthwani deserves systems that work. Here is my contract with you."

Infrastructure That Works For All

  • Roads: Upgrading of main and feeder roads in Utawala Airways/Githunguri, Manyatta/Mayland, Seme/Katani, Joska, Kamulu, Ngelani, Kavilila/Misuuni Market, Ivalini/Ndovoini & Ng'alalya.
  • Water: Constant coordination between Mavwasco and Athi water works development agency for fresh water provision.
  • Lighting: "Mulika Mwizi" for all estates.
  • Public Institutions: Primary and secondary schools, Hospital & level 2 and 3 dispensaries, Library and Sports Utility Grounds.

Muthwani Financial Empowerment Program (MFEP)

  • Household-Level Income Growth: Practical support to help families turn skills, talents, and small enterprises into reliable income.
  • Job Placement, Skills Training & Upskilling : Develop a system that enlists jobless individuals with opportunities within Mavoko Constituency. .
  • Micro-Enterprise Support: Mentorship, market access linkages, and structured support for small businesses, men and women groups, youth people living with disabilities groups.
  • Digital & Future Skills: Introducing Muthwani to digital opportunities, online work, and emerging skill sets that are shaping the global economy.

Leadership That Is Responsive, Accountable & Innovative

  • Responsive Leadership: Commited to ensure that every voice is heard and every concern is acted upon quickly and respectfully.
  • Accountable Governance: We pledge clear and transparent reporting on actions taken.
  • Innovative Problem Solving: Challenges facing muthwani require new thinking, we will embrace mordern solutions, smart service delivery & community led innovation to solve issues in infrastructure, economic empowerment and in social services.
  • Leadership Commitment: To lead with integrity, manage public resources with discipline and build a future where development is predictable.
